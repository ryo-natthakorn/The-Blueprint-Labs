Scroll slowly. The wheel is already turning.

中心 02 / 07

Centering

The clay fights you at first. Steady pressure, wet palms — until it runs true and silent under the hand. Nothing can be built on a lump that wobbles.

開く 03 / 07

Opening

Thumbs find the centre and press down. A floor forms. The walls remember every hesitation, so we don't hesitate.

引き上げ 04 / 07

Pulling up

Three pulls, no more. Knuckle outside, fingertips inside, rising together. Each pass leaves a ring in the wall — this is the moment most pots die.

形 05 / 07

Shaping

A rib against the shoulder coaxes the belly outward. The curve you're watching was decided a thousand pots ago; the hand just remembers it.

窯 06 / 07

Into the kiln

Bone-dry, it rests for a week, then fires for fourteen hours. At 1280°C the clay turns to stone, shrinks by a tenth, and the glaze melts into glass.

器 07 / 07

Fired & finished

Studio celadon, pooling deep green in every throwing ring. Drag the vessel to turn it in your hands.
