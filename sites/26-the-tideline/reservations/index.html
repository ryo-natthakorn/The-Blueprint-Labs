Reservations

A table by the water, held in your name.

Four quick steps. Tables are released sixty days out, the dining room seats forty, and the bar is always kept free for walk-ins. Parties of eight or more head to our private room instead.

How many of you?

Tables in the dining room seat up to seven. Eight or more is a private-room night, and a better night, honestly.

Eight or more: let's do this properly.

Our private dining room sits just off the main kitchen and seats up to fourteen: same menu, same sourcing, a table that's actually yours for the night. Chef's tasting for six and up; full buyouts for larger events.
