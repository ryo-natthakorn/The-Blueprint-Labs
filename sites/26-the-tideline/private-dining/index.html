What's included

Same kitchen, your table

  • A welcome pour at the bar while your table is finished.
  • The night's menu chalked on your own wall, dishes named for the occasion if you like.
  • Chef's tasting for six or more: seven courses, decided that morning at the dock.
  • Wine pairings or the zero-proof flight, matched course by course.
  • Dietary needs folded into the menu, never bolted on.

How it works

Three emails, no contracts by the pound

  1. 1

    Tell us the occasion

    Date, headcount and anything the table needs to know: allergies, anniversaries, someone who claims they hate fish. Use the enquiry form or just call.

  2. 2

    We hold the room

    You'll hear back within a day with a menu sketch for the season and a simple per-head figure. One deposit holds the night.

  3. 3

    The tide does the rest

    The final menu is written the morning of, same as the dining room. That's not a caveat; it's the reason to come.
